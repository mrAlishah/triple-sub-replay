# TripleSub Replay v2.8.0 — Tabs, subtitle controls and Anki

[← TripleSub Replay README](../../README.md) · [All releases](README.md) · [Fork changelog](../../myCHANGELOG.md)

**Published:** October 27, 2026 (UTC) · **[Release and APK](https://github.com/mrAlishah/triple-sub-replay/releases/tag/v2.8.0)**

## What's new

### Browse with multiple YouTube tabs

Open up to **10 tabs**. Switch between videos, close a tab, and restore tab state when restarting the app. Supported devices may also allow up to three separate Google accounts via compatible WebView profiles.

### Customize all three subtitle slots

The three roles are **Original Subtitle**, **Subtitle 1** and **Subtitle 2**. Configure each slot's order, size and alignment. Persian/RTL layout, floating-panel positioning and fullscreen rotation were improved.

### Better Anki compatibility

This release documented the **TripleSub Replay v3** AnkiDroid note type, with `Word`, `Subtitle 1`, `Subtitle 2`, `Reading`, `Sentence`, `Translation`, `Text`, `Source` and `Online Example`. Older saved words and Anki v2 notes remain intact.

### Update links

The app's update-information area gained links for both **TripleSub Replay** and original **DualSub Replay** releases.
