# AI Assistant Chat

[← User guide](README.md) · [AI translation](ai-translation.md)

TripleSub Replay provides an optional AI Assistant for questions about the app, language learning, words and subtitle sentences. Which providers and controls are available depends on the installed build.

## Start a conversation

1. Open the **AI Assistant** control in the app.
2. If required, visit its **Settings / AI Connection** to choose and authenticate your **Chat AI Service**.
3. Ask a question such as **“Explain the current subtitle line”** or **“How can I change subtitle size?”**.
4. Send a follow-up question to continue the conversation.

In some customized builds you can enable **Use memory in this chat**, manage history, and inspect response-source information.

## AI identity and providers

The assistant belongs to **TripleSub Replay**, a customized fork of **DualSub Replay**. If a provider requires API-key authentication, use that provider's official credentials. **ChatGPT Subscription** sign-in is separate from OpenAI API-key billing; availability depends on the installed fork build and account eligibility.

The **Chat AI Service** should not change your independently configured **Translation AI Service**.

## Examples

- “Why is my second translated line blank?”
- “Explain this German sentence in simple English.”
- “How do I use Practice due words?”
- “What does this expression mean in context?”

Review AI answers critically, particularly grammatical explanations and translations. The selected provider processes what you send according to its own terms.
