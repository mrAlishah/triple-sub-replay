# Getting started with TripleSub Replay

[← User guide](README.md) · [Download the customized APK](https://github.com/mrAlishah/triple-sub-replay/releases/latest)

## Install

1. On your Android phone (Android **8.0 or later**), visit [TripleSub Replay Releases](https://github.com/mrAlishah/triple-sub-replay/releases/latest).
2. Open **Assets** and download **`TripleSub-Replay.apk`**. The original `DualSub-Replay.apk` is a different app distribution.
3. Open the downloaded file. If Android requests it, allow the browser/file manager to install apps.
4. Tap **Install**, then **Open**.

**Updating:** install the newer fork APK over your copy only when both its package ID and signing certificate match. If Android says the package conflicts, **do not uninstall first**. Export your Practice backup and check [troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).

## Watch your first video

1. Open TripleSub Replay and choose a YouTube video that has captions.
2. Bring up the subtitle panel beneath the video.
3. Open **Subtitle languages** (the CC/language control).
4. Choose **Original Subtitle**, **Subtitle 1** and optionally **Subtitle 2**.
5. If captions have not arrived, allow a moment for YouTube caption discovery; some videos do not offer usable tracks.

## Learn from a sentence

- Read the three aligned lines in the subtitle panel.
- To replay a segment, press its **Play** button. Newer development builds avoid seeking when you tap the card background.
- Select a word for lookup and the available Copy, Translate and Pronounce actions.
- Save a word or phrase, then open Practice to review it.

## Quick settings

**Languages** controls the three subtitle slots and language choices. **Captions & translation** controls Google, AI and on-device translation. **AI Connection** (in applicable builds) manages configured AI services.

Use [Triple subtitles](triple-subtitles.md) for display details and [Translation and AI](ai-translation.md) for modes and service requirements.

## What the app does not do

The YouTube page plays the video: TripleSub Replay is not a video-downloading tool. Online translation and AI features require appropriate network/provider access. Actual availability depends on the installed release.
